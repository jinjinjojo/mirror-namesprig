How the filters work

Ranks are within each sex, so a name can be #5 for girls and #900 for boys. "Mostly girls" means more than half of the babies given the name in the last ten years were girls; "unisex" means each sex had at least a third. "Peaked in" is the decade of the single year the most babies got the name. The 10-year trend compares the name's rank in 2025 with 2015. Names given to fewer than 5 babies in a year aren't in Social Security's files, so very rare spellings aren't here. Counts are sourced from the SSA files; ranks, the girl/boy split and the trend are computed from those counts. Nothing is estimated.
