Ariel by year, 1880 to 2025 Sourced

In 2025, 851 girls and 533 boys born in the US were named Ariel, ranking it #356 for girls. That's down 219 places since 2015.

The name peaked in 1991, when 6,077 babies got it (#66 for girls). Since 1880, Social Security has recorded 100,665 babies with the name, 78% of them girls. First recorded in 1892.

How the Ariel figures are worked out

Babies per year are Social Security's own counts. The rank is Ariel's place among girls's names by babies in 2025. The peak is the year the most babies of either sex got it. The total adds up every year since 1880; years when fewer than 5 babies of a sex got the name aren't in the files, so it is a floor. A state's rate is its Ariel babies per 1,000 girls listed in that state.
