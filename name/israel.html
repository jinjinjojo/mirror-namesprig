In 2025, 42 girls and 1,081 boys born in the US were named Israel, ranking it #315 for boys. That's down 75 places since 2015.

The name peaked in 2007, when 1,995 babies got it (#204 for boys). Since 1880, Social Security has recorded 72,874 babies with the name, 2% of them girls. First recorded in 1880.

How the Israel figures are worked out

Babies per year are Social Security's own counts. The rank is Israel's place among boys's names by babies in 2025. The peak is the year the most babies of either sex got it. The total adds up every year since 1880; years when fewer than 5 babies of a sex got the name aren't in the files, so it is a floor. A state's rate is its Israel babies per 1,000 boys listed in that state.
