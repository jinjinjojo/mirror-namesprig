In 2025, 444 girls and 41 boys born in the US were named Shelby, ranking it #649 for girls. That's down 349 places since 2015.

The name peaked in 1991, when 10,744 babies got it (#33 for girls). Since 1880, Social Security has recorded 165,279 babies with the name, 90% of them girls. First recorded in 1880.

How the Shelby figures are worked out

Babies per year are Social Security's own counts. The rank is Shelby's place among girls's names by babies in 2025. The peak is the year the most babies of either sex got it. The total adds up every year since 1880; years when fewer than 5 babies of a sex got the name aren't in the files, so it is a floor. A state's rate is its Shelby babies per 1,000 girls listed in that state.
