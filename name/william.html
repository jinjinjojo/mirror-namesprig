In 2025, 7 girls and 10,545 boys born in the US were named William, ranking it #9 for boys. That's down 4 places since 2015.

How the William figures are worked out

Babies per year are Social Security's own counts. The rank is William's place among boys's names by babies in 2025. The peak is the year the most babies of either sex got it. The total adds up every year since 1880; years when fewer than 5 babies of a sex got the name aren't in the files, so it is a floor. A state's rate is its William babies per 1,000 boys listed in that state.
