River by year, 1880 to 2025 Sourced

In 2025, 1,401 girls and 3,139 boys born in the US were named River, ranking it #111 for boys. That's up 133 places since 2015.

The name peaked in 2022, when 5,430 babies got it (#105 for boys). Since 1880, Social Security has recorded 59,351 babies with the name, 33% of them girls. First recorded in 1971.

How the River figures are worked out

Babies per year are Social Security's own counts. The rank is River's place among boys's names by babies in 2025. The peak is the year the most babies of either sex got it. The total adds up every year since 1880; years when fewer than 5 babies of a sex got the name aren't in the files, so it is a floor. A state's rate is its River babies per 1,000 boys listed in that state.
