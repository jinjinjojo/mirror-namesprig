Sydney by year, 1880 to 2025 Sourced

In 2025, 873 girls and 20 boys born in the US were named Sydney, ranking it #348 for girls. That's down 226 places since 2015.

The name peaked in 2000, when 10,369 babies got it (#23 for girls). Since 1880, Social Security has recorded 187,700 babies with the name, 95% of them girls. First recorded in 1880.

How the Sydney figures are worked out

Babies per year are Social Security's own counts. The rank is Sydney's place among girls's names by babies in 2025. The peak is the year the most babies of either sex got it. The total adds up every year since 1880; years when fewer than 5 babies of a sex got the name aren't in the files, so it is a floor. A state's rate is its Sydney babies per 1,000 girls listed in that state.
