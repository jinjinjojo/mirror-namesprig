Among kids born in 2025, Mel would show up one in about every 4,912 classes of 25. At its peak in 1958 it was about 1 in 31,318, so one in about every 1,253 classes of 25. These are national averages for one birth year; in states where the name is more common the odds run higher.

In 2025, 16 girls and 11 boys born in the US were named Mel, outside the top 1,000 for boys.

The name peaked in 1958, when 132 babies got it (#689 for boys). Since 1880, Social Security has recorded 4,201 babies with the name, 12% of them girls. First recorded in 1902.

How the Mel figures are worked out

Babies per year are Social Security's own counts. The rank is Mel's place among boys's names by babies in 2025. The peak is the year the most babies of either sex got it. The total adds up every year since 1880; years when fewer than 5 babies of a sex got the name aren't in the files, so it is a floor. A state's rate is its Mel babies per 1,000 boys listed in that state.
