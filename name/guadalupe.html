Guadalupe by year, 1880 to 2025 Sourced

In 2025, 180 girls and 24 boys born in the US were named Guadalupe, outside the top 1,000 for girls. It dropped out of the top 1,000 since 2015.

The name peaked in 1997, when 1,656 babies got it (#210 for girls). Since 1880, Social Security has recorded 91,997 babies with the name, 73% of them girls. First recorded in 1880.

How the Guadalupe figures are worked out

Babies per year are Social Security's own counts. The rank is Guadalupe's place among girls's names by babies in 2025. The peak is the year the most babies of either sex got it. The total adds up every year since 1880; years when fewer than 5 babies of a sex got the name aren't in the files, so it is a floor. A state's rate is its Guadalupe babies per 1,000 girls listed in that state.
