In 2025, 16 girls and 768 boys born in the US were named Jaden, ranking it #421 for boys. That's down 220 places since 2015.

The name peaked in 2007, when 7,256 babies got it (#74 for boys). Since 1880, Social Security has recorded 95,059 babies with the name, 14% of them girls. First recorded in 1974.

How the Jaden figures are worked out

Babies per year are Social Security's own counts. The rank is Jaden's place among boys's names by babies in 2025. The peak is the year the most babies of either sex got it. The total adds up every year since 1880; years when fewer than 5 babies of a sex got the name aren't in the files, so it is a floor. A state's rate is its Jaden babies per 1,000 boys listed in that state.
