Sawyer by year, 1880 to 2025 Sourced

In 2025, 1,056 girls and 2,941 boys born in the US were named Sawyer, ranking it #122 for boys. That's down 28 places since 2015.

The name peaked in 2017, when 5,562 babies got it (#95 for boys). Since 1880, Social Security has recorded 84,176 babies with the name, 22% of them girls. First recorded in 1913.

How the Sawyer figures are worked out

Babies per year are Social Security's own counts. The rank is Sawyer's place among boys's names by babies in 2025. The peak is the year the most babies of either sex got it. The total adds up every year since 1880; years when fewer than 5 babies of a sex got the name aren't in the files, so it is a floor. A state's rate is its Sawyer babies per 1,000 boys listed in that state.
