Micah by year, 1880 to 2025 Sourced

In 2025, 291 girls and 3,817 boys born in the US were named Micah, ranking it #90 for boys. That's up 15 places since 2015.

The name peaked in 2024, when 4,246 babies got it (#86 for boys). Since 1880, Social Security has recorded 133,606 babies with the name, 11% of them girls. First recorded in 1946.

How the Micah figures are worked out

Babies per year are Social Security's own counts. The rank is Micah's place among boys's names by babies in 2025. The peak is the year the most babies of either sex got it. The total adds up every year since 1880; years when fewer than 5 babies of a sex got the name aren't in the files, so it is a floor. A state's rate is its Micah babies per 1,000 boys listed in that state.
