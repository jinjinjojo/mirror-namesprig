Frankie by year, 1880 to 2025 Sourced

In 2025, 549 girls and 256 boys born in the US were named Frankie, ranking it #922 for boys. That's up 65 places since 2015.

The name peaked in 1959, when 1,198 babies got it (#268 for boys). Since 1880, Social Security has recorded 79,430 babies with the name, 47% of them girls. First recorded in 1880.

How the Frankie figures are worked out

Babies per year are Social Security's own counts. The rank is Frankie's place among boys's names by babies in 2025. The peak is the year the most babies of either sex got it. The total adds up every year since 1880; years when fewer than 5 babies of a sex got the name aren't in the files, so it is a floor. A state's rate is its Frankie babies per 1,000 boys listed in that state.
