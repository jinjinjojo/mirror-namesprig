In 2025, 393 girls and 32 boys born in the US were named Leslie, ranking it #712 for girls. That's down 341 places since 2015.

How the Leslie figures are worked out

Babies per year are Social Security's own counts. The rank is Leslie's place among girls's names by babies in 2025. The peak is the year the most babies of either sex got it. The total adds up every year since 1880; years when fewer than 5 babies of a sex got the name aren't in the files, so it is a floor. A state's rate is its Leslie babies per 1,000 girls listed in that state.
