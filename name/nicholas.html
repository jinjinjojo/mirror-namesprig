Nicholas by year, 1880 to 2025 Sourced

In 2025, 8 girls and 3,115 boys born in the US were named Nicholas, ranking it #112 for boys. That's down 50 places since 2015.

The name peaked in 1995, when 29,216 babies got it (#7 for boys). Since 1880, Social Security has recorded 929,199 babies with the name, under 1% of them girls. First recorded in 1880.

How the Nicholas figures are worked out

Babies per year are Social Security's own counts. The rank is Nicholas's place among boys's names by babies in 2025. The peak is the year the most babies of either sex got it. The total adds up every year since 1880; years when fewer than 5 babies of a sex got the name aren't in the files, so it is a floor. A state's rate is its Nicholas babies per 1,000 boys listed in that state.
