In 2025, 3,362 girls and 551 boys born in the US were named Eden, ranking it #70 for girls. That's up 86 places since 2015.

The name peaked in 2025, when 3,913 babies got it (#70 for girls). Since 1880, Social Security has recorded 63,400 babies with the name, 83% of them girls. First recorded in 1917.

How the Eden figures are worked out

Babies per year are Social Security's own counts. The rank is Eden's place among girls's names by babies in 2025. The peak is the year the most babies of either sex got it. The total adds up every year since 1880; years when fewer than 5 babies of a sex got the name aren't in the files, so it is a floor. A state's rate is its Eden babies per 1,000 girls listed in that state.
