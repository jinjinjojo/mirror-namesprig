In 2025, 245 girls and 908 boys born in the US were named Spencer, ranking it #360 for boys. That's down 108 places since 2015.

How the Spencer figures are worked out

Babies per year are Social Security's own counts. The rank is Spencer's place among boys's names by babies in 2025. The peak is the year the most babies of either sex got it. The total adds up every year since 1880; years when fewer than 5 babies of a sex got the name aren't in the files, so it is a floor. A state's rate is its Spencer babies per 1,000 boys listed in that state.
