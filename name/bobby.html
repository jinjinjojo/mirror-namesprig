In 2025, 12 girls and 195 boys born in the US were named Bobby, outside the top 1,000 for boys. It dropped out of the top 1,000 since 2015.

The name peaked in 1935, when 7,971 babies got it (#24 for boys). Since 1880, Social Security has recorded 324,728 babies with the name, 3% of them girls. First recorded in 1900.

How the Bobby figures are worked out

Babies per year are Social Security's own counts. The rank is Bobby's place among boys's names by babies in 2025. The peak is the year the most babies of either sex got it. The total adds up every year since 1880; years when fewer than 5 babies of a sex got the name aren't in the files, so it is a floor. A state's rate is its Bobby babies per 1,000 boys listed in that state.
