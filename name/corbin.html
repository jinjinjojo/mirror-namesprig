In 2025, 9 girls and 586 boys born in the US were named Corbin, ranking it #512 for boys. That's down 291 places since 2015.

The name peaked in 2014, when 1,943 babies got it (#203 for boys). Since 1880, Social Security has recorded 41,777 babies with the name, 2% of them girls. First recorded in 1891.

How the Corbin figures are worked out

Babies per year are Social Security's own counts. The rank is Corbin's place among boys's names by babies in 2025. The peak is the year the most babies of either sex got it. The total adds up every year since 1880; years when fewer than 5 babies of a sex got the name aren't in the files, so it is a floor. A state's rate is its Corbin babies per 1,000 boys listed in that state.
