Jackson by year, 1880 to 2025 Sourced

In 2025, 15 girls and 6,582 boys born in the US were named Jackson, ranking it #36 for boys. That's down 19 places since 2015.

The name peaked in 2013, when 12,626 babies got it (#16 for boys). Since 1880, Social Security has recorded 297,998 babies with the name, under 1% of them girls. First recorded in 1880.

How the Jackson figures are worked out

Babies per year are Social Security's own counts. The rank is Jackson's place among boys's names by babies in 2025. The peak is the year the most babies of either sex got it. The total adds up every year since 1880; years when fewer than 5 babies of a sex got the name aren't in the files, so it is a floor. A state's rate is its Jackson babies per 1,000 boys listed in that state.
