In 2025, 156 girls and 414 boys born in the US were named Layne, ranking it #662 for boys. That's up 49 places since 2015.

How the Layne figures are worked out

Babies per year are Social Security's own counts. The rank is Layne's place among boys's names by babies in 2025. The peak is the year the most babies of either sex got it. The total adds up every year since 1880; years when fewer than 5 babies of a sex got the name aren't in the files, so it is a floor. A state's rate is its Layne babies per 1,000 boys listed in that state.
