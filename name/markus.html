How the Markus figures are worked out

Babies per year are Social Security's own counts. The rank is Markus's place among boys's names by babies in 2025. The peak is the year the most babies of either sex got it. The total adds up every year since 1880; years when fewer than 5 babies of a sex got the name aren't in the files, so it is a floor. A state's rate is its Markus babies per 1,000 boys listed in that state.
