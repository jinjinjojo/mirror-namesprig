London by year, 1880 to 2025 Sourced

In 2025, 719 girls and 144 boys born in the US were named London, ranking it #431 for girls. That's down 326 places since 2015.

The name peaked in 2013, when 3,879 babies got it (#85 for girls). Since 1880, Social Security has recorded 54,779 babies with the name, 81% of them girls. First recorded in 1886.

How the London figures are worked out

Babies per year are Social Security's own counts. The rank is London's place among girls's names by babies in 2025. The peak is the year the most babies of either sex got it. The total adds up every year since 1880; years when fewer than 5 babies of a sex got the name aren't in the files, so it is a floor. A state's rate is its London babies per 1,000 girls listed in that state.
