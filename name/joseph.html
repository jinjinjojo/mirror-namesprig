Joseph by year, 1880 to 2025 Sourced

In 2025, 12 girls and 7,303 boys born in the US were named Joseph, ranking it #29 for boys. That's down 8 places since 2015.

The name peaked in 1956, when 32,832 babies got it (#12 for boys). Since 1880, Social Security has recorded 2,680,213 babies with the name, under 1% of them girls. First recorded in 1880.

How the Joseph figures are worked out

Babies per year are Social Security's own counts. The rank is Joseph's place among boys's names by babies in 2025. The peak is the year the most babies of either sex got it. The total adds up every year since 1880; years when fewer than 5 babies of a sex got the name aren't in the files, so it is a floor. A state's rate is its Joseph babies per 1,000 boys listed in that state.
