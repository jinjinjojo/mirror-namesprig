Devon by year, 1880 to 2025 Sourced

In 2025, 44 girls and 216 boys born in the US were named Devon, outside the top 1,000 for boys. It dropped out of the top 1,000 since 2015.

The name peaked in 1995, when 4,001 babies got it (#119 for boys). Since 1880, Social Security has recorded 92,976 babies with the name, 22% of them girls. First recorded in 1914.

How the Devon figures are worked out

Babies per year are Social Security's own counts. The rank is Devon's place among boys's names by babies in 2025. The peak is the year the most babies of either sex got it. The total adds up every year since 1880; years when fewer than 5 babies of a sex got the name aren't in the files, so it is a floor. A state's rate is its Devon babies per 1,000 boys listed in that state.
