In 2025, 41 girls and 253 boys born in the US were named Dillon, ranking it #930 for boys. That's down 482 places since 2015.

The name peaked in 1992, when 5,082 babies got it (#73 for boys). Since 1880, Social Security has recorded 66,270 babies with the name, 2% of them girls. First recorded in 1892.

How the Dillon figures are worked out

Babies per year are Social Security's own counts. The rank is Dillon's place among boys's names by babies in 2025. The peak is the year the most babies of either sex got it. The total adds up every year since 1880; years when fewer than 5 babies of a sex got the name aren't in the files, so it is a floor. A state's rate is its Dillon babies per 1,000 boys listed in that state.
