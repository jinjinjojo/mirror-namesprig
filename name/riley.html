Riley by year, 1880 to 2025 Sourced

In 2025, 4,441 girls and 1,666 boys born in the US were named Riley, ranking it #48 for girls. That's down 13 places since 2015.

The name peaked in 2008, when 9,906 babies got it (#39 for girls). Since 1880, Social Security has recorded 246,985 babies with the name, 57% of them girls. First recorded in 1880.

How the Riley figures are worked out

Babies per year are Social Security's own counts. The rank is Riley's place among girls's names by babies in 2025. The peak is the year the most babies of either sex got it. The total adds up every year since 1880; years when fewer than 5 babies of a sex got the name aren't in the files, so it is a floor. A state's rate is its Riley babies per 1,000 girls listed in that state.
