Dylan by year, 1880 to 2025 Sourced

In 2025, 338 girls and 6,208 boys born in the US were named Dylan, ranking it #41 for boys. That's down 14 places since 2015.

The name peaked in 2001, when 16,696 babies got it (#21 for boys). Since 1880, Social Security has recorded 434,951 babies with the name, 4% of them girls. First recorded in 1953.

How the Dylan figures are worked out

Babies per year are Social Security's own counts. The rank is Dylan's place among boys's names by babies in 2025. The peak is the year the most babies of either sex got it. The total adds up every year since 1880; years when fewer than 5 babies of a sex got the name aren't in the files, so it is a floor. A state's rate is its Dylan babies per 1,000 boys listed in that state.
