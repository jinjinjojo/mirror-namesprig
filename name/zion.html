Zion by year, 1880 to 2025 Sourced

In 2025, 94 girls and 2,496 boys born in the US were named Zion, ranking it #142 for boys. That's up 85 places since 2015.

The name peaked in 2023, when 3,020 babies got it (#125 for boys). Since 1880, Social Security has recorded 53,897 babies with the name, 13% of them girls. First recorded in 1917.

How the Zion figures are worked out

Babies per year are Social Security's own counts. The rank is Zion's place among boys's names by babies in 2025. The peak is the year the most babies of either sex got it. The total adds up every year since 1880; years when fewer than 5 babies of a sex got the name aren't in the files, so it is a floor. A state's rate is its Zion babies per 1,000 boys listed in that state.
