How the Jose figures are worked out

Babies per year are Social Security's own counts. The rank is Jose's place among boys's names by babies in 2025. The peak is the year the most babies of either sex got it. The total adds up every year since 1880; years when fewer than 5 babies of a sex got the name aren't in the files, so it is a floor. A state's rate is its Jose babies per 1,000 boys listed in that state.
