Elliot by year, 1880 to 2025 Sourced

In 2025, 241 girls and 2,417 boys born in the US were named Elliot, ranking it #151 for boys. That's up 57 places since 2015.

The name peaked in 2017, when 2,932 babies got it (#161 for boys). Since 1880, Social Security has recorded 65,600 babies with the name, 11% of them girls. First recorded in 1880.

How the Elliot figures are worked out

Babies per year are Social Security's own counts. The rank is Elliot's place among boys's names by babies in 2025. The peak is the year the most babies of either sex got it. The total adds up every year since 1880; years when fewer than 5 babies of a sex got the name aren't in the files, so it is a floor. A state's rate is its Elliot babies per 1,000 boys listed in that state.
