In 2025, 2,887 girls and 6 boys born in the US were named Maria, ranking it #87 for girls. That's up 22 places since 2015.

The name peaked in 1964, when 10,197 babies got it (#41 for girls). Since 1880, Social Security has recorded 570,389 babies with the name, 99% of them girls. First recorded in 1880.

How the Maria figures are worked out

Babies per year are Social Security's own counts. The rank is Maria's place among girls's names by babies in 2025. The peak is the year the most babies of either sex got it. The total adds up every year since 1880; years when fewer than 5 babies of a sex got the name aren't in the files, so it is a floor. A state's rate is its Maria babies per 1,000 girls listed in that state.
