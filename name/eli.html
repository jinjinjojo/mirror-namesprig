Eli by year, 1880 to 2025 Sourced

In 2025, 35 girls and 3,418 boys born in the US were named Eli, ranking it #104 for boys. That's down 51 places since 2015.

The name peaked in 2012, when 8,110 babies got it (#43 for boys). Since 1880, Social Security has recorded 154,512 babies with the name, 1% of them girls. First recorded in 1880.

How the Eli figures are worked out

Babies per year are Social Security's own counts. The rank is Eli's place among boys's names by babies in 2025. The peak is the year the most babies of either sex got it. The total adds up every year since 1880; years when fewer than 5 babies of a sex got the name aren't in the files, so it is a floor. A state's rate is its Eli babies per 1,000 boys listed in that state.
