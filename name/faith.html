In 2025, 1,212 girls and 8 boys born in the US were named Faith, ranking it #254 for girls. That's down 164 places since 2015.

How the Faith figures are worked out

Babies per year are Social Security's own counts. The rank is Faith's place among girls's names by babies in 2025. The peak is the year the most babies of either sex got it. The total adds up every year since 1880; years when fewer than 5 babies of a sex got the name aren't in the files, so it is a floor. A state's rate is its Faith babies per 1,000 girls listed in that state.
