Jersey by year, 1880 to 2025 Sourced

In 2025, 157 girls and 47 boys born in the US were named Jersey, outside the top 1,000 for girls.

The name peaked in 2010, when 242 babies got it. Since 1880, Social Security has recorded 4,673 babies with the name, 86% of them girls. First recorded in 1948.

How the Jersey figures are worked out

Babies per year are Social Security's own counts. The rank is Jersey's place among girls's names by babies in 2025. The peak is the year the most babies of either sex got it. The total adds up every year since 1880; years when fewer than 5 babies of a sex got the name aren't in the files, so it is a floor. A state's rate is its Jersey babies per 1,000 girls listed in that state.
