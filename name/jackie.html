In 2025, 86 girls and 62 boys born in the US were named Jackie, outside the top 1,000 for girls.

How the Jackie figures are worked out

Babies per year are Social Security's own counts. The rank is Jackie's place among girls's names by babies in 2025. The peak is the year the most babies of either sex got it. The total adds up every year since 1880; years when fewer than 5 babies of a sex got the name aren't in the files, so it is a floor. A state's rate is its Jackie babies per 1,000 girls listed in that state.
