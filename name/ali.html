In 2025, 44 girls and 1,116 boys born in the US were named Ali, ranking it #306 for boys. That's up 19 places since 2015.

The name peaked in 2014, when 1,251 babies got it (#334 for boys). Since 1880, Social Security has recorded 44,888 babies with the name, 23% of them girls. First recorded in 1914.

How the Ali figures are worked out

Babies per year are Social Security's own counts. The rank is Ali's place among boys's names by babies in 2025. The peak is the year the most babies of either sex got it. The total adds up every year since 1880; years when fewer than 5 babies of a sex got the name aren't in the files, so it is a floor. A state's rate is its Ali babies per 1,000 boys listed in that state.
