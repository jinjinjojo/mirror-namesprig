Joey by year, 1880 to 2025 Sourced

In 2025, 146 girls and 274 boys born in the US were named Joey, ranking it #877 for boys. That's down 119 places since 2015.

The name peaked in 1962, when 1,572 babies got it (#200 for boys). Since 1880, Social Security has recorded 61,402 babies with the name, 12% of them girls. First recorded in 1915.

How the Joey figures are worked out

Babies per year are Social Security's own counts. The rank is Joey's place among boys's names by babies in 2025. The peak is the year the most babies of either sex got it. The total adds up every year since 1880; years when fewer than 5 babies of a sex got the name aren't in the files, so it is a floor. A state's rate is its Joey babies per 1,000 boys listed in that state.
