In 2025, 4,657 girls and 149 boys born in the US were named Nova, ranking it #46 for girls. That's up 167 places since 2015.

How the Nova figures are worked out

Babies per year are Social Security's own counts. The rank is Nova's place among girls's names by babies in 2025. The peak is the year the most babies of either sex got it. The total adds up every year since 1880; years when fewer than 5 babies of a sex got the name aren't in the files, so it is a floor. A state's rate is its Nova babies per 1,000 girls listed in that state.
