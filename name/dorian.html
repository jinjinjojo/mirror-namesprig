In 2025, 7 girls and 557 boys born in the US were named Dorian, ranking it #539 for boys. That's up 21 places since 2015.

The name peaked in 2000, when 703 babies got it (#398 for boys). Since 1880, Social Security has recorded 28,728 babies with the name, 16% of them girls. First recorded in 1915.

How the Dorian figures are worked out

Babies per year are Social Security's own counts. The rank is Dorian's place among boys's names by babies in 2025. The peak is the year the most babies of either sex got it. The total adds up every year since 1880; years when fewer than 5 babies of a sex got the name aren't in the files, so it is a floor. A state's rate is its Dorian babies per 1,000 boys listed in that state.
