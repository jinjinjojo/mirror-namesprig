In 2025, 1,331 girls and 198 boys born in the US were named Wren, ranking it #231 for girls. That's up 476 places since 2015.

The name peaked in 2022, when 1,830 babies got it (#184 for girls). Since 1880, Social Security has recorded 15,902 babies with the name, 86% of them girls. First recorded in 1888.

How the Wren figures are worked out

Babies per year are Social Security's own counts. The rank is Wren's place among girls's names by babies in 2025. The peak is the year the most babies of either sex got it. The total adds up every year since 1880; years when fewer than 5 babies of a sex got the name aren't in the files, so it is a floor. A state's rate is its Wren babies per 1,000 girls listed in that state.
