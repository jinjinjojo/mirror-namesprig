In 2025, 14 girls and 7,230 boys born in the US were named Alexander, ranking it #30 for boys. That's down 22 places since 2015.

How the Alexander figures are worked out

Babies per year are Social Security's own counts. The rank is Alexander's place among boys's names by babies in 2025. The peak is the year the most babies of either sex got it. The total adds up every year since 1880; years when fewer than 5 babies of a sex got the name aren't in the files, so it is a floor. A state's rate is its Alexander babies per 1,000 boys listed in that state.
