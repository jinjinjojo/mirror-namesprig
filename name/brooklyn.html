Brooklyn by year, 1880 to 2025 Sourced

In 2025, 2,300 girls and 37 boys born in the US were named Brooklyn, ranking it #118 for girls. That's down 87 places since 2015.

The name peaked in 2011, when 7,257 babies got it (#21 for girls). Since 1880, Social Security has recorded 127,055 babies with the name, 99% of them girls. First recorded in 1972.

How the Brooklyn figures are worked out

Babies per year are Social Security's own counts. The rank is Brooklyn's place among girls's names by babies in 2025. The peak is the year the most babies of either sex got it. The total adds up every year since 1880; years when fewer than 5 babies of a sex got the name aren't in the files, so it is a floor. A state's rate is its Brooklyn babies per 1,000 girls listed in that state.
