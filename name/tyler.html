In 2025, 101 girls and 1,633 boys born in the US were named Tyler, ranking it #220 for boys. That's down 139 places since 2015.

How the Tyler figures are worked out

Babies per year are Social Security's own counts. The rank is Tyler's place among boys's names by babies in 2025. The peak is the year the most babies of either sex got it. The total adds up every year since 1880; years when fewer than 5 babies of a sex got the name aren't in the files, so it is a floor. A state's rate is its Tyler babies per 1,000 boys listed in that state.
