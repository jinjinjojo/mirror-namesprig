In 2025, 10 girls and 6,569 boys born in the US were named Gabriel, ranking it #37 for boys. That's down 15 places since 2015.

The name peaked in 2008, when 13,188 babies got it (#24 for boys). Since 1880, Social Security has recorded 402,627 babies with the name, 2% of them girls. First recorded in 1880.

How the Gabriel figures are worked out

Babies per year are Social Security's own counts. The rank is Gabriel's place among boys's names by babies in 2025. The peak is the year the most babies of either sex got it. The total adds up every year since 1880; years when fewer than 5 babies of a sex got the name aren't in the files, so it is a floor. A state's rate is its Gabriel babies per 1,000 boys listed in that state.
