In 2025, 12,754 girls and 14 boys born in the US were named Emma, ranking it #3 for girls. That's down 2 places since 2015.

The name peaked in 2003, when 22,756 babies got it (#2 for girls). Since 1880, Social Security has recorded 778,172 babies with the name, over 99% of them girls. First recorded in 1880.

How the Emma figures are worked out

Babies per year are Social Security's own counts. The rank is Emma's place among girls's names by babies in 2025. The peak is the year the most babies of either sex got it. The total adds up every year since 1880; years when fewer than 5 babies of a sex got the name aren't in the files, so it is a floor. A state's rate is its Emma babies per 1,000 girls listed in that state.
