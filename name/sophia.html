In 2025, 12,561 girls and 11 boys born in the US were named Sophia, ranking it #5 for girls. That's down 2 places since 2015.

The name peaked in 2012, when 22,354 babies got it (#1 for girls). Since 1880, Social Security has recorded 439,619 babies with the name, over 99% of them girls. First recorded in 1880.

How the Sophia figures are worked out

Babies per year are Social Security's own counts. The rank is Sophia's place among girls's names by babies in 2025. The peak is the year the most babies of either sex got it. The total adds up every year since 1880; years when fewer than 5 babies of a sex got the name aren't in the files, so it is a floor. A state's rate is its Sophia babies per 1,000 girls listed in that state.
