Kendall by year, 1880 to 2025 Sourced

In 2025, 932 girls and 163 boys born in the US were named Kendall, ranking it #328 for girls. That's down 181 places since 2015.

The name peaked in 2011, when 3,004 babies got it (#124 for girls). Since 1880, Social Security has recorded 101,883 babies with the name, 65% of them girls. First recorded in 1906.

How the Kendall figures are worked out

Babies per year are Social Security's own counts. The rank is Kendall's place among girls's names by babies in 2025. The peak is the year the most babies of either sex got it. The total adds up every year since 1880; years when fewer than 5 babies of a sex got the name aren't in the files, so it is a floor. A state's rate is its Kendall babies per 1,000 girls listed in that state.
