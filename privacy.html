Privacy policy

Last updated 2026-10-22.

What we collect

We count page views and a few interactions (searches, outbound clicks) to see which pages help people and how people move between the sites in our network. We record the page, the site that sent you, your device type, browser, country (from our host's network headers) and any campaign tag in the link. We don't store your IP address: the server uses it only to make a once-a-day code that can't be turned back into the address or linked to another day, and we use no device fingerprinting.

Outside the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland, unless you opt out below, we keep a random, anonymous visitor id in your browser's local storage and pass it along when you follow a link to another of our sites, so we can count one visit across sites. In the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland we store nothing in your browser unless you allow it below. Without it, each site only sees a daily count that can't be linked to you, to another day or to another site. If your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, nothing is counted at all.

Signing in

Signing in is optional; everything on Namesprig that doesn't need an account works without it. Signing in uses one account shared by the sites in our network: you sign in with an email code or a passkey, and we never see a password, only your email and an account id for this site. When you sign in we link your visitor id (if you allowed it) to your account, so your visits on different sites and devices join up. On your account page you can download that data or delete your account, which deletes it everywhere.

What you post

  • Comments, posts and forum threads are public. They show the name you chose with your account and link to your public profile, with the time you posted. Don't post anything you want kept private.
  • Moderators can see everything posted, including edits and anything reported. Some posts are held for a moderator to look at before they show.
  • You can edit or delete your own posts. Deleting your account removes your posts or detaches them from your name.

Payments

Where Namesprig offers paid features (a tip, an unlock, a membership, a purchase), a card payment is taken on Stripe's payment page. We never see or keep your card number. We keep a record of what was bought, the amount, the date and whether it was refunded, because we need it to give you what you paid for and to keep accounts. See the refund page.

Reports

If you use Report a problem, we get the page address, your note, your browser type, any errors the page logged and, only if you add them, a screenshot and an email address. We use them to look into the report and reply. We keep a hashed code of your connection for a short time to stop spam.

Things saved in your browser

This site doesn't set cookies. Your settings (such as light or dark mode) and saved items stay in your browser's local storage, along with your sign-in. They stay on your device; clear your site data to remove them. Other companies' pages you are sent to, such as Stripe's payment page, have their own rules.

Who else handles your data

  • Our web host, which serves the pages and sees your connection like any host does.
  • Our visit-counting service, described above.
  • Our account service, which keeps your email and account id.
  • Stripe, for card payments.
  • OpenStreetMap, on pages that show a map: it serves the map pictures and so sees your connection.
  • We don't sell your data.

Ads and affiliate links

Some pages have space for ads. None of them use cookies. Some links are affiliate links: if you buy something after following one, we may earn a commission at no cost to you. They're marked.

Your choices

Opting out deletes the visitor id from your browser.

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to see, correct or delete the data we hold about you. Ask us and we'll help.

Children

Namesprig is not aimed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data. If you think a child has given us data, tell us and we'll delete it.

Changes and contact

If we change this page, the date above changes. Questions or a request to see or delete data: the Report a problem link at the bottom of every page.
