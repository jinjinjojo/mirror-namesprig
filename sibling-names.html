Sibling Name Finder

Enter your older child's name and get brother and sister names from the same era and about as popular.

Data: Social Security card applications, 1880 to 2025

SSA files dated Apr 13, 2026

Sourced

How it works

Names feel like a set when they come from the same era and are about as common. The finder looks up your child's name in 6,092 names from US records, finds the decade it peaked and how popular it is, and suggests sisters and brothers that match both. It runs in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere.

How the matches are scored

Each candidate gets a distance: the gap between its peak decade and your child's, in decades, plus 1.5 times the gap in how many babies have had each name (on a log scale, so 10,000 and 100,000 are one step apart), plus 1 if it isn't in this year's top 1,000. The 12 lowest win. Names that start with the same letter are left out so the set doesn't sound alike. Sisters are names at least 80% girls since 1880; brothers at least 80% boys. This is a rule of thumb, not a measure of taste.
