What the numbers mean

Share of births is babies given the name per 10,000 babies of both sexes recorded that year, so years with more births don't look more popular. Rank is within girls or boys for 2025. 10-year trend compares the rank with 2015. States counts where Social Security lists the name in 2025; it lists a name in a state only when 5 or more babies of that sex got it. Babies, peak births and totals are sourced counts; shares, ranks, trends, girl/boy splits and state rates are computed from them. Nothing is estimated. Full method and sources.
