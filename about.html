About Namesprig

What this site is

Look up, filter, shortlist and compare US baby names, with popularity by year and state since 1880 from Social Security records.

Where the data comes from

Every page is built from open data. We credit each source:

  • Source: U.S. Social Security Administration, baby names from Social Security card applications (Public domain (US government work, 17 U.S.C. 105))
